The source code of MILE is free software under the [AGPL-3.0](LICENSE). The **name "MILE"** and the **MILE logo** (the letter M drawn as a road, in every color and size) are **not** covered by that license: they identify the official project maintained by Gabriele Menghi. All rights on them are reserved.

## Allowed without asking

- Running the official, unmodified releases and calling them MILE.
- Referring to the project by name, e.g. in articles, reviews, tutorials, comparisons, or "works with MILE".
- Packaging the official releases unmodified (e.g. in an app catalog such as TrueNAS or Unraid), keeping the name and logo, as long as it is clear that the package is not made by the maintainer.

## Requires written permission

- Distributing a **modified version** (a fork) under the name MILE or with the MILE logo. Forks are welcome, but must use a different name and logo that cannot be confused with MILE, and must remove the MILE branding from the interface.
- Offering MILE, or a modified version of it, as a **hosted service** under the MILE name.
- Using the name or logo in a product name, domain name, company name or in a way that suggests endorsement by, or affiliation with, the project.

To ask for permission, open an issue on https://github.com/mile-garage/mile.
